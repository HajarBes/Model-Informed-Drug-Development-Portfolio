# Citations — Project 05: Sotorasib Exposure-Response

## Primary Sources

### 1. FDA Label & Clinical Pharmacology Review
- **Sotorasib (LUMAKRAS) FDA Label.** Prescribing Information, revised 2023.
  - CL/F = 26.2 L/hr (CV 76%), Vd/F = 211 L (CV 135%), t½ = 5 hr
  - AUC0-24h,ss (960 mg) = 65.3 hr·µg/mL, Cmax,ss = 7.50 µg/mL
  - Protein binding: 89% (fu = 0.11)

### 2. Population PK Model
- **Nagase S, et al.** Population pharmacokinetics of sotorasib in patients with KRAS p.G12C-mutated advanced solid tumors. *AAPS J.* 2025.
  - 2-compartment model with 3 transit absorption compartments
  - Covariates on CL: ECOG, tumor size, albumin (significant)
  - Not significant: weight, age, sex, race, renal function

### 3. CodeBreaK 100 Phase 1 Dose Escalation
- **Hong DS, et al.** KRAS G12C inhibition with sotorasib in advanced solid tumors. *N Engl J Med.* 2020;383(13):1207-1217.
  - Dose escalation: 180, 360, 720, 960 mg QD
  - ORR by dose: 33% (180 mg), 25% (360 mg), 50% (720 mg), 47% (960 mg)

### 4. CodeBreaK 100 Phase 2
- **Skoulidis F, et al.** Sotorasib for lung cancers with KRAS p.G12C mutation. *N Engl J Med.* 2021;384(25):2371-2381.
  - ORR (BICR) = 36% at 960 mg, N=124
  - Median PFS = 6.8 months

### 5. Dose Comparison Study
- **de Langen AJ, et al.** Sotorasib versus docetaxel for previously treated non-small-cell lung cancer with KRAS G12C mutation: a randomised, open-label, phase 3 trial. *Lancet.* 2023.
- **Sotorasib 240 mg vs 960 mg dose comparison.** *Eur J Cancer.* 2024.
  - 960 mg (N=104): ORR 32.7%, median PFS 5.4 mo, median OS 13.0 mo
  - 240 mg (N=105): ORR 24.8%, median PFS 5.6 mo, median OS 11.7 mo

### 6. Safety / Hepatotoxicity
- **FDA Clinical Review, BLA 761232.** Safety analyses, hepatotoxicity section.
  - Grade 3+ hepatotoxicity: 16% at 960 mg (pooled)
  - Prior CPI ≤30 days: 75% hepatotoxicity rate
  - Prior CPI >90 days: 0% hepatotoxicity rate

### 7. Regulatory & Editorial Commentary
- **FDA Project Optimus.** Dose optimization initiative for oncology products. 2023.
- **Patel MR, Forde PM.** Editorial: Sotorasib dosing — less may be more. *J Clin Oncol.* 2024.
- **ICH E4.** Dose-response information to support drug registration. 1994.
- **FDA Guidance.** Exposure-response relationships — study design, data analysis, and regulatory applications. 2003.
- **FDA Draft Guidance.** Optimizing the dosage of human prescription drugs and biological products for the treatment of oncologic diseases. 2023.
