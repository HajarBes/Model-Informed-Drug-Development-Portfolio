# Context of Use — Sotorasib Exposure-Response & Dose Optimization

## Regulatory Context

### FDA Dose Optimization Initiative (Project Optimus)
In 2023, FDA launched Project Optimus to ensure that oncology drug doses are optimized, not just maximized. The initiative emphasizes that the historical approach of selecting the maximum tolerated dose (MTD) may not yield the optimal benefit-risk balance. Sotorasib is a central case study for this initiative.

### Post-Marketing Requirement (PMR)
Following accelerated approval of sotorasib at 960 mg QD, the FDA mandated a post-marketing requirement (PMR) to evaluate 240 mg versus 960 mg. This reflects concern that the approved dose may be higher than necessary given the flat dose-exposure relationship from saturable absorption.

### ODAC Vote (October 2023)
The Oncologic Drugs Advisory Committee voted 10-2 that the available evidence did not support the conclusion that 960 mg provides a clinically meaningful benefit over 240 mg. This vote highlighted the regulatory significance of exposure-response analysis in dose selection.

## Why Sotorasib Is a Compelling E-R Case Study

1. **Saturable absorption** creates a pharmacokinetic ceiling — exposure barely changes from 180 to 960 mg
2. **Flat dose-response** is not failure — it's the pharmacological consequence of saturation
3. **Active regulatory debate** — FDA PMR, ODAC vote, JCO editorials
4. **Safety signal** — hepatotoxicity linked to both exposure and prior CPI, creating an actionable risk factor
5. **Demonstrates the harder analytical skill** — "what do you do when E-R is flat and the dose may be wrong?"

## Intended Use

This analysis is a **methodological demonstration** for portfolio purposes. It illustrates the application of E-R analysis and dose optimization using published clinical data. It is not a regulatory filing and does not contain proprietary or individual patient-level data.
